Sideline Shove

Surf the ball carrier into the crowd. Move players to close off squares, then chain the pushes.

How the rules work here Tap one of your players. Blue squares are where it can move (M5, no GFIs). There are no dodges: a player can step into an opposing tackle zone, but once it is standing in one it can't move again, so it can only block from there. Tap a red-ringed opponent next to it to block. Uphill blocks (opponent picks the dice) are not allowed, so spare players have to move in and mark the defenders who are giving defensive assists; a ✕ chip shows a blocked-off block. You can switch this off with the checkbox. Block alone = Block action. Move first then block = Blitz (only one per turn, the block costs 1 move, a Frenzy second block costs 1 more). A player who only moves is a Move action. Every block is assumed to be a push; the chip shows the block dice and the real odds of getting a push are shown at the end.

Pushed players choose an empty square among the three behind them. If none are empty but one is crowd, they go into the crowd. If all three are occupied, the pushed player shoves one of them in turn, which is pushed away from it. Squares outside the view are walls. Frenzy: the first push forces a follow-up, then a second block is made if the target is still on the pitch and that block isn't uphill.
