Rerolls are spent on the first failures in the chain, in order. Once they're used up, the next failure ends the sequence. Block dice: 2 or 3 dice are chosen by the attacker, −2 or −3 dice by the defender. “Push required” accepts any result except Attacker Down and Both Down. On 2D6, “8+” means 8 or higher: armour is broken on its value or better, so 8+ covers 8 to 12. Casualty results follow the D16 table: 1–8 Badly Hurt, 9–10 Seriously Hurt, 11–12 Serious Injury, 13–14 Lasting Injury, 15–16 Dead. A Lasting Injury then rolls a D6 for the reduction (1–2 −1 AV, 3 −1 MV, 4 −1 PA, 5 −1 AG, 6 −1 ST). To chain them, add a Casualty roll, then a Lasting Injury roll.
